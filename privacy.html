Privacy Policy

Dungeon Legends · dungeons.reillyl.com · Last updated October 14, 2026

Dungeon Legends is a free study game. You can play without an account using Play Offline, in which case nothing is sent to us and your progress stays in your browser.

What we collect when you sign in with Google

  • Your Google account ID and email address, used only to sign you in.
  • Your first name, used to create your in-game username (for example "Alex" or "Alex2").
  • Your game save: progress, items, settings, and the study decks you create or import.
  • Basic activity: your deepest floor reached and when you last played.

How it is used

  • To save your progress so it follows you between devices.
  • To show the leaderboard. Only your username and deepest floor are shown to other players. Your email is never shown.
  • To let the game's admin remove cheating or abusive accounts.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone except the services that run the game: Supabase (database and sign-in) and Google (sign-in). The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Deleting your data

Ask the developer to delete your account, using the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Dungeon Legends. Your profile, save, and leaderboard entry will be removed.
